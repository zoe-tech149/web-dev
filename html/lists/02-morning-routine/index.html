Morning Routine

  1. Wake up: Turn off the alarm and get out of bed.

  2. Hydrate: Drink a large glass of water.

  3. Make the bed: Straighten the sheets and pillows for an instant sense of accomplishment.

  4. Hygiene: Brush teeth, wash face, shower, and get dressed.

  5. Meditation: Spend 5 to 10 minutes clearing your mind or loosening up muscles.

  6. Review daily goals: Check your calendar or to-do list for the day's top priorities.
